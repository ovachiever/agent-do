## Verdict in one line

The plan is not a bigger taste costume. It is a taste **abstention** wrapped around a large engineering chassis: a typed layout DSL, a browser observer, a dependency-aware repair loop, and evidence hygiene, with the host LLM in the generator seat and an LLM critic in the reviewer seat. It contains no beauty claim, no scorer, and no variation engine. It moves dpt toward Erik's goal on one axis only (honest floors, modern-color measurement, coverage receipts) and quietly redefines the goal on the other two.

## C. Is it a generative grammar?

**No. It is a typed composition DSL with a validator and a repair loop, and an LLM in the generator seat.** Judged against Stiny-style shape grammars (vocabulary, spatial relations, rewriting rules, a derivation, nondeterministic rule choice, a generated language):

- **Vocabulary:** yes, 16 operators as a closed typed AST (04:172-189) plus a closed expression language (04:196-205). Terminals only.
- **Non-terminals / rewriting rules:** none. The eight recipes F-01..F-08 are macros: "Recipes in the worked examples are compositions of existing operators and do not extend this closed executable vocabulary" (04:542). No production of the form `region → partition(region, region)`; no recursive expansion; the word "derivation" appears only for arithmetic (04:44), breakpoints (04:216), and fixture math (09:221).
- **Who generates:** "Host AI proposes a composition program" (03:15); "The host proposes an AST and frames from the brief, content graph and available operator contracts" (05:185-186); "The host model is an explicit creative participant" (03:29-30). The core "validates and lowers" (03:13). The grammar is therefore a **schema the LLM must write inside**, not a system that produces designs from constraints.
- **Stochastic choice:** a seeded sampler exists, "keyed by `(seed, stable_choice_id, counter)`" (05:207), but "Sampling only proposes values inside declared domains" (02:133) and "Repeatability of the host model's original proposal is a different, unpromised property" (05:212). The seed jitters parameters inside domains the model already chose. Structure is never sampled.
- **Repair step:** yes, and it is the plan's strongest engineering: typed `PatchOperation`s (04:349-350), base-digest refusal, dependency closure, stale-observation invalidation, cycle and oscillation detection (05:311-344). Constraint infeasibility yields a named conflict, not fabricated dimensions (02:45-47, 05:117-139). This is real.
- **Constraint relaxation:** none. "Hard requirements remain constraints on any deliverable. Selected construction choices can be replaced by a new valid program" (05:332-333), and replacement is host-driven.
- **"Never the same thing twice":** handled by measurement and refusal, not mechanism. "Diversity is reported as changed decisions; there is no novelty reward" (05:204-205); "A diversity report documents variation; it never rewards strangeness for its own sake" (07-EVALUATION-AND-GENERALIZATION.md:176); "Record repeated topologies and whether the brief explains them" (07:165-166). The only production-side lever is asking the host for "distinct composition programs" (01:31; 07:97-100).

The record already measured what happens when the raw prior is the generator: "the two independent nulls per brief converged so tightly that blind judges described each pair as 'one habit'… The sameness attractor is not a hypothesis; it is now a measured property of the raw prior" (dpt-moon:68). The plan puts that prior in the generator seat, gives it a schema, and reports sameness afterwards. The record's own countermeasure, the P4 museum ("Quality-diversity archive: cells over grammar families… Never-twice falsifier: after 10 briefs, no two occupants judged same-family", dpt-moon:55), is not cited and has no successor in the plan.

## D. Evaluation (07)

**Pre-registered in form, wrong ground truth, missing the two traps the record made mandatory, and two AI-judge seats left open.**

Good: claims are separated ("Engineering completion and demonstrated aesthetic benefit are separate release statements. Neither is allowed to inherit the other's evidence", 07:16-17); the manifest is frozen before outputs (07:45-57); "No significance threshold is tuned after seeing the outcome" (05:407); prospective briefs after the grammar freeze are the strongest test (07:36-39); the baseline is non-straw (07:113-117); ablations remove reference retrieval, repair, and visual review (07:126-130); missingness stays visible (07:151-154); the engineering gate is against its own rules and says so, the benefit gate is against human preference (07:180-192). Not circular.

Defects, each citable:

1. **Ground truth is "human", not Erik.** "Actual human preferences" (07:13), "Human judgment and accounting" (07:132), "Ask for a preference, tie, conditional preference or neither" (07:139), and Erik appears only as "Author acceptance: Erik's actual acceptance, if required by the commissioned launch" (07:194). The ruling is that his eye is the only ratifying authority (les-4fa9be; les-3d0e14: "'Ratified' means Erik ruled on it at his decision bar, nothing else"). With any other raters the study becomes a population preference model, which Erik set aside.
2. **No Round-2 trap.** The wild-hunt protocol's gate (c) — "it must NOT crown the heavy conceits Erik hated" (wild-hunt.md:17-19) — does not appear. Since the plan has no scorer, the trap's subject would be the plan's two AI judges: the visual critic (ALG-06, 05:277-287: "The critic identifies a concrete observation, its task consequence, a proposed change") and delegated selection (01:37-38; `selection_authority: author|delegated_host`, 04:80; "`observed → delivered` is permitted only when the brief delegates selection", 04:415). Neither is ever shown the concept2 set to check whether it prefers the Null over the conceits. ADR-16 names the risk in the abstract ("drift toward the critic's favorite style", 10:27) without the evidence that it already happened twice.
3. **No register-blindness gate.** Gate (d) — "read all five pins as high-structure despite their wildly different registers" (wild-hunt.md:19-20) — has no successor. Pins serve only as retrieval-holdout groups (07:27-34) and "an audit of range" (07:41-43). The obvious adaptation is missing: run the 71-rule inspector over all twelve pins once COL-01/02 close the parser gap and require that no H-failure fires that Erik's eye would overrule (the P1 audit found dpt's floors were not register-blind: sr02 on inline links, ts09 hero ratio, dpt-audit:38-41). Eight of twelve pins are INCOMPLETE today (corpus/README.md:28; 11:48); the plan's own observer is the fix, and the pins are its acceptance test. Not proposed.
4. **Delegated selection is AI-judge authority with an honest label.** "Such selection is recorded as model selection, never human approval" (01:38) keeps the record truthful, but a model choosing what ships is the thing les-4fa9be forbids for taste-driven deliverables. It should be off in any Erik-facing run.
5. **The comparison that matters is not proposed.** The workflow that already works is design-rounds ("his feedback compiled into binding constraints, N mutually-blind builders spanning a range within them, dpt floors as annotations", les-4fa9be). The plan treats mn-7ec6dc as "an optional consumer of these immutable runs" (03:187-188). The decisive experiment is instrument-inside-rounds versus rounds-alone, judged by Erik. Not in 07.

## E. No-preaching and word-ration as output constraints

**Tooling leak is forbidden; doctrine-as-copy is not; the word ration is explicitly de-lawed.**

- What is covered: "Internal rule IDs and instrumentation do not leak into product UI" (01:19); "The exported website does not expose internal rule IDs, tooling status, or design-analysis prose to its end users" (06-FRESH-SESSION-AND-INTEGRATION.md:63-64). REQ-05's "copy audit" (01:78) is about copying *from references* ("borrowed page copy", 07:163-166), not about pages narrating themselves.
- What is open: the IntentFrame admits "sanctuary, workshop, observatory, or marketplace" as author language (01:98-99); `symbolic_readings` is a first-class field (04:114); "The esoteric ambition is supported through explicit symbolic readings" (01:113-115); the worked example's intent is a phrase ("practical exchange with the care of a field notebook", 09:161-162). Nothing stops the host from setting that phrase as the H1. Today's kill was exactly that: "All five candidates preached the doctrine to the user in headlines and subtitles… The pages narrated their own epistemology instead of being a room" (les-b23e92). INT-04 makes it worse in tendency by demanding a rationale per character choice (02:153): more explaining, not less.
- Word ration: listed among rules "we deliberately do not encode": "a fixed word budget" (02:314), and "The system does not invent global asset, word or node ceilings" (02:263). Available only "if the brief calls for them" (02:315). For a general tool that is defensible; for Erik's surfaces every brief must now carry the ration and the no-preaching law by hand, and the DesignBrief has only `prohibited_effects: string[]` (04:80) to carry them, with no check behind the strings.
- The substrate for a mechanical check exists and is unused: every `ContentNode` carries `provenance: Authority` (04:90) and INT-01 maps generated content to brief requirements (02:150). A locked brief requirement "generated meaningful text: provenance=proposed, counted, and ≤ N where N is the brief's ration; no text node may restate an IntentFrame phrase" would be a P-class rule one line long. It is not there.

## G. Overall

This plan does not repeat P1's failure. It contains no taste costume because it contains no taste claim: no scorer, no beauty number, no style law, no AI verdict in Erik's name, all stated in the plan's own text (02:3-4, 02:310-320, 03:126-129, 04:384-387). What it builds instead is a large, careful chassis: a typed layout DSL the model must write inside, a browser observer that refuses to fake completeness, a dependency-aware repair loop, and evidence hygiene modeled on this repo's own doctrine. Measured against Erik's two stated goals it moves one axis and quietly abandons two. It moves "honest floors" forward (modern color, composited backgrounds, coverage receipts, typed unknowns). It abandons "AI determines beauty through maths and geometry" by design and says so only indirectly, restating the target as "consistently strong original design from a fresh capable session" (11:23-24) and calling "always awesome" "an ambition, not a demonstrated guarantee" (11:24-25). And it abandons "never the same thing twice" to the raw prior whose sameness attractor is already measured (dpt-moon:68), offering a diversity report and a parameter-jitter seed where the record's own answer was a quality-diversity museum with a never-twice falsifier (dpt-moon:55). The word "grammar" is doing rhetorical work; the thing is a DSL plus a validator.

**Keep:** the "rules we deliberately do not encode" list (02:310-320) as standing law for all future dpt work; the four-class H/G/J/P scheme and REP-02's no-aggregate rule (02:12-20, :270) as doctrine; the `Observed` known/unknown/not_applicable contract (04:36-41) and COL-01/COL-02 (02:203-204), ported into dpt now to close the INCOMPLETE-pins gap honestly; ALG-08's typed patches and dependency closure (05:311-344) as the one genuinely new engineering idea, built after the pilot; the nineteen relation signatures (04:557-577) as critic inputs only, never summed, and only once a critic has passed the Round-2 trap; the claims separation and non-straw baseline in 07 (07:16-19, :113-117).

**Drop or defer:** the 11-verb tool and registry entry (06:66-84) until benefit evidence exists; WP-05/06 completeness; symbolic readings; the generality that de-laws the word ration and leaves no-preaching unchecked (02:314, 04:80), replaced by a one-line P rule over `ContentNode.provenance`; "human" as ground truth, replaced by Erik.

**One-paragraph answer to "does it improve dpt the way Erik wants":** partly, and not where he pointed. It would make dpt's floor stratum honest and generator-aware, which the record says is worth doing and cheap in its first slice. It would not give silicon a way to see beauty, and it would not stop the prior from producing the same page twice; it replaces both with process. The record's next real move on taste is still the one the plan does not cite: design-rounds with Erik's eye as the only judge, a museum that refuses same-family entries, and any new computed candidate walking through the four-gate kill-test before it touches a score.
