## Verdict in one line

Aphrodite is the wild hunt's unity-in-variety, three-trees, size-chord, transport and squint houses rewritten as smooth kernels with context-supplied targets, multiplied together, and placed under a preference-learning layer whose load-bearing inputs (the semantic graph, the salience adapter, the material-meaning descriptors, the intention binding) are model-authored. It is a much better-shaped plan than its predecessor: a real functional, real productions, the critic out of the selection seat, no-preaching typed into the renderer. It is also, by its own text, a program with no kill condition, no pre-registered prediction on the existing record, and no definition that could be computed on a fixture today.

### A.2 What g, t, c are

The worked synergy (05:9-13) defines g, t and c as *manipulations* ("how the geometry groups and distinguishes the roles; how the available type treatments are assigned to those roles; how the available material/color relationships support that grouping") and then assigns them fixture values 0.2 and 0.9 (05:26). No function from a rendered page to g exists anywhere in the package. The plan says so: "The numbers below are exact illustrative fixtures for the proposed model. They are not measurements from the twelve pins" (05:3-4); VALIDATION.json:150 labels the arithmetic "proposed-model arithmetic only". The mixed-difference identity (05:47-53) holds for any product of three numbers. It establishes that the plan's combiner *can* express a three-way interaction, which was never in doubt.

### A.3 Is there a theoretical reason for multiplication?

None is stated. The plan's justification is that "the term `gamma*g*t*c` has a nonzero mixed finite difference" (01:267-268), which is the definition of an interaction, not a reason to expect one, and the plan concedes it: "This is a place to encode emergent beauty, not evidence that this particular product is the right human beauty law" (05:56-57). The choice does carry substantive content the plan does not name, and it is testable: a product of affinities in `(0,1]` is a **weakest-link conjunction**. It prefers `(0.6, 0.6, 0.6)` (product 0.216) over `(0.9, 0.9, 0.1)` (product 0.081) where a sum prefers the second. The hypothesis is therefore "Erik's eye is limited by the weakest channel." The corpus's own reading of the pins argues the opposite: "conviction and a disciplined chassis; volume varies freely" (corpus/README.md:27), Sneak "a stark black-and-white chassis, hyper-saturated product art carrying ALL the color" (pin 005), every.to "true black, crisp white, a contemporary serif, generous air" (pin 002). Those read as one channel driven hard while others are held flat, the one-spike profile of wild-hunt #28 (wild-hunt.md:398-410), which a product penalizes and a max rewards. The plan should say which it believes before fitting, because a fitted `gamma` will not tell it.

Note also that the first model is additive across families, `B_seed = (F_01 + F_02 + F_03 + F_08) / 4` (04:19), with interaction living only inside `F_02` and `F_08`. The milestone's "joint versus additive" comparison (04:35-37) is therefore "a sum with two product-valued features versus a sum with marginal features". Fine, but it should be described that way.

### A.4 The Three Trees repair

This is the plan's best-grounded move and it deserves precision. Three Trees scored min over three normalized MI agreements between Ward clusterings of position, of style, and DOM containment (PROTOCOL.md:138-149; metrics.py:103-124). It went 8/10 with both Null traps passing (RESULTS.md:98: +0.093, +0.077). Its two misses were paneldock vs statled (gap −0.0095) and paneldock vs quiet (gap −0.1201) (scores.json, `gates.18_trees.B`), and its fixture ordering was correct by 0.0018 against a 0.05 margin (RESULTS.md:41). The plan's repair (05:113-134; AP-T03 at 04:175) replaces "three partitions forced to be identical" with typed edges that may cross. Two things follow. First, the repair's central input is no longer the DOM but a semantic graph, and 03:31 puts the host model in charge of writing it: "The host model interprets the brief into proposed content/role relationships." Whoever writes the edges writes the score. For historic pages someone must author those edges by hand or by model before the repaired kernel can be evaluated at all. Second, the miss against `quiet` by 0.12 is the largest gap in the table and it is a consonance measure preferring the quieter page, which is the pattern the audit named (dpt-audit:54: "Poverty reads as discipline to a variance metric"). The repair has to fix that specific miss with targets declared in advance, or it has repaired nothing.

## B. The barf test

**Under the plan's own definitions, the plan cannot say what barf scores, and the record says it will score high.** The word "barf" appears in the package once, in AP-T14: "Null/conceits, canons/barf/broken and all available pins remain visible by group/support" (04:186). "Remain visible" is not a prediction.

Reasoning through the four active families with the plan's text: barf (fixtures/README.md:11: a disciplined neon template, 17-icon sidebar, two font sizes per dpt-audit:54) gets a semantic graph with many `same_role` edges (sidebar items, cards) and tight `belongs_to` groups. APH-01 rewards repeated treatment on `same_role` and proximity on `belongs_to` (01:135-136): high. APH-03 with two font sizes has zero chord residual under any correspondence: maximal. APH-02's U (shared identity) is at its ceiling on a template. APH-08 multiplies high affinities. Nothing in chapter 1 predicts a low value for barf unless the "proposed" targets are written against it, which would be fitting to the control. This is not speculation about the family; it is the record for every consonance-type proxy already tried: Three Trees barf 0.3695 vs canon-dossier 0.3713 (RESULTS.md:41), Size chord barf best of four (:47), HTML Self-Teaching barf best (:43), Emphasis transport barf best (:45). The plan's reply to the Three Trees fixture result is "Positive ordering and weak separation coexist ... do not describe this as reversed ordering" (02:17), which keeps the ordering and drops the margin.

**Blank white image.** This one the plan handles. An empty part set is "inapplicable, not a perfect score" (01:80), "Required content missing from the graph makes the composition ineligible" (01:81), and `total` is absent when required input is missing (03:161). That closes the TSE hole (RESULTS.md:11, :126). What it does not close is the minimal-content cousin: one heading, one paragraph, one perfect `belongs_to` edge. Because "relationship weights sum to one within their family" (01:78-79), a page with one perfect edge ties a page with forty perfect edges on that family, and the anti-degeneracy clause guards only the other direction ("More relationships do not mean more beauty", 01:338-339). Fewer relations, all satisfied, is the attractor. That is barf's shape.

**Maximalist versus quiet pins.** Pete's Pasta and Sneak (corpus/README.md:26) versus Attio and Things (:22). The plan asserts the outcome it wants: "A dense illustrated page and a spare textual page can both have high U and D" (01:154-155); "Density is purposeful, not a penalty" (05:150). Neither is derived. Under any repetition-favoring target Attio's monochrome grotesk beats Pete's badges, tickers and mascots on APH-01/02/03. The plan cannot say otherwise and does not try.

**Register-blindness gate.** There is none. Gate D of the protocol, all pins above max(barf, broken) by margin (PROTOCOL.md:92-95), has no successor. AP-WP-04 makes the pins "an evidence and support audit, with incomplete captures still incomplete" (04:141-142) and refuses the four gates outright: "The review recommends ... requiring every new candidate to survive the old four-gate screen. Those recommendations conflict with the user's current direction and are not adopted" (02:207-209). Two problems. The work order's source line is "take wild-hunt results rather than conclusions" (mn-eafd32:5). The gates *are* the results: barf's value, the Null's value, the pins' values are measurements; "KILLED" was the conclusion. Declining to inherit the label does not license declining to run the measurement. And mechanically, the pins cannot be scored under this plan at all: every APH family needs a semantic graph and DOM descriptors, the five archived pins have "No matched historic DOM" (rounds/pins/labels.yaml, `dom_absent_reason`), the seven newer pins have 2026-09-14 shots and no probes (`corpus/shots/`, zero pin entries in `.dev/dpt-wildhunt/probes/`), and the plan forbids substituting modern DOMs (02:18). So gate D is not merely unscheduled, it is unrunnable until someone captures live pin DOMs, which the plan does not schedule.

Under the plan's own rule that B ranks only "alternatives under the same bound context" (01:83-84), the usable count is **10 strict, 5 unconditional, at most 26 counting descriptive pairs**. The plan knows the structure ("not ten fully independent human decisions", 04:197-198). It does not state the number.

**How many free parameters.** The plan says "The initial four coefficients and diagonal scales avoid a high-capacity fit" (04:199-200). The four coefficients are the estimated layer. The capacity is in the "proposed" layer beneath them: per semantic relation kind (eight listed at 03:185) × channel (four: g, t, p, m at 01:129-130) × descriptor (03:180-183 lists roughly seven geometry, five typography, five paint, five material) × `(mu, sigma)` × modes (at least one, "several harmonic arrangements" at 01:106). At the thinnest reading, one descriptor per channel per kind, one mode, diagonal, that is 8 × 4 × 2 = **64 proposed kernel parameters**; at the descriptor lists as written it is several hundred; APH-03 adds `a_i, b_i` per bound dimension and APH-08 adds the constellation catalog. A proposed parameter that may be "repaired" when a counterexample appears (02:41-42: "Historical failures become localized counterexamples: a mapping to repair, a relation that needs conditioning") is a fitted parameter with no ledger of its degrees of freedom. Four coefficients on ten rows is identifiable in count (rank ≤ 4); sixty-plus revisable targets on ten rows will fit anything, and the plan's own diagnostic ("Report the rank and conditioning of the actual feature-difference matrix", 02:108) reports the first number, not the second.

Read plainly: with the data that exist and the data the first milestone can produce, whether the joint model "helped" will be decided by the author looking at the numbers. That is the reviewer in the decision seat, wearing a fit.

The factorial design in 04:207-211 is the right instrument and the same arithmetic applies: eight cells per brief, one rater, one ordinal response per cell, and the three-way human interaction the plan wants to detect (05:44-53) is a single number per brief with no variance estimate. It needs many briefs, and each brief is Erik's time.

## D. Generation

**This time there is a grammar.** GR-01 to GR-09 (03:244-262) have a start symbol `Page(S)`, nonterminals carrying role sets and obligations (03:245-247), six structural productions (Partition, Sequence, Cluster, Field, Overlay, plus terminal, treatment and reflow), a termination argument (03:263-267), a bounded diverse beam (03:271), and the sentence the previous plan lacked: "A host proposal is an optional additional derivation, not the only way a graph comes into existence" (03:274-275). AP-T11 tests it (04:183). Credit where due: this answers section C of the earlier review.

Two limits. The structural vocabulary is five region operators; every derivation is a tree of splits, lists, lead/support, anchor/satellites and overlays. That is the vocabulary of every layout engine, and it is where the medium's sameness lives (dpt-moon:68). Structure varies; character does not come from structure. Character comes from GR-08 `SharedFrame(identity, role_variants)` deriving "type, paint, spacing and material differences from joint variables" (03:259), and the joint variables' domains and the material "mode catalog" (03:198-200) are authored, "proposed" lists. At the material level this is parameter jitter over authored modes, the earlier plan's seed sampler under a new name (03:301-304 keys the seed stream by production path and choice id; the earlier one keyed it by choice id). Half a grammar: real at the layout level, authored at the level Erik's eye actually reads.

**Sameness counter-mechanism.** Named, and it is the P4 museum's descendant without the citation: a local archive of accepted derivation signatures and a structural-distance constraint for requested variants (03:306-317). Two caveats in the plan's own text. Distance is applied only among candidates "within a declared beauty tolerance of the best eligible candidate" (03:308-310), so variety is bounded by the width of B's maximum, and B's four active families all reward repetition and consonance. And "If no distinct candidate exists within the budget/beauty region, report that result" (03:315): the mechanism's failure mode is refusal, which the plan elsewhere correctly counts as a coverage failure (02:146-149; 04:215). Whether the archive ever admits a second family is therefore an empirical property of B, untested. The never-twice falsifier from the moon program ("after 10 briefs, no two occupants judged same-family", dpt-moon:55) is the right test and is not adopted; AP-T12 checks that the distance contract is followed, not that it produces anything.

Repair: kept and improved. Three levels of transformation evidence (03:344-350), the dependency graph extended to joint factors ("Changing a palette can invalidate material resonance ... even when no DOM box moved", 03:357-360), stale-B cannot select (AP-T10, 04:182). This remains the plan's strongest engineering.

## F. Circularity

The nine laws were written by a model that had read the twelve pins, all rounds, RESULTS.md and the prior review (02:192-194 says so). The question is whether anything can now falsify them.

What the plan freezes: the model before each generation ("fixed before the candidate is optimized", 01:321-322) and before each judged batch (02:112); and it states the right rule for used-up test sets ("Once a batch informs a model change, it becomes development data for that successor", 04:203-204). What it does not do: pre-register any prediction on the existing record. All historical material is declared development data ("Use the historical material openly for development", 02:159), the four gates are declined (02:207-209), and the only prospective holdout is "genuinely new briefs and outputs" (02:160) produced by the same model and judged joint-versus-additive against each other, never against the fixed record.

Then the exits. "If higher-order features contribute no useful gain over the simpler models, revise or replace those features" (04:147-148): revise, not stop. "The whole project is not rejected because every metric does not simultaneously win every test" (02:167-168). "Neither an old scalar failure nor one new loss mandates abandoning the program" (04:143). "Historical failures become localized counterexamples: a mapping to repair" (02:41-42), with no cap on repairs. Put together: no result on the record can falsify a law, because the record is development data; no result on new briefs can falsify a law, because a miss is a mapping to repair; and the program has no stated kill condition. The plan calls this "Evaluation that develops rather than destroys the program" (02:158). That is the finding. A research program that cannot lose is not a research program, and the wild-hunt protocol's whole value was that it could (PROTOCOL.md:97-102). The correct amendment is small and the plan already has the vocabulary for it: declare the proposed targets, then run the record as a *diagnostic* and publish the four numbers before any generator exists (section G, WP-0).

One honest thing to say for the plan: its Holm arithmetic (02:34-39) is right that gate B demanded 9/10 on ten dependent pairs, which was harsh. But gate A was a margin, not a p-value, and barf inside 0.0018 of canon is what killed Three Trees regardless of Holm. The plan's argument addresses the gate it can win and not the one it lost.
