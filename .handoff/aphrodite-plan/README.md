# Aphrodite: a generative mathematics of relational beauty

Corrected plan, 2026-09-14. Commission: `mn-eafd32`. This supersedes the product objective
and build order of `mn-27e068`. The earlier composition machinery is supporting infrastructure.

**Aphrodite must compute and generate beauty.** Its central object is a mathematical model of
how relationships make a composition beautiful, including interactions that cannot be reduced
to the separate quality of its parts. That model selects and changes designs. A language-model
critic does not occupy the decision seat in its place.

The earlier plan answered how to make valid, coherent, inspectable websites. It explicitly
excluded the requested beauty model and was therefore aimed at the wrong product. Its contracts,
browser observations and dependency handling can be reused when they serve the new core.

## The design in one paragraph

Represent a page as a graph of meaningful visual parts, explicit empty regions, typed
relationships and configurations involving several parts. Encode proposed beauty laws as
smooth compatibility functions over those relationships. Couple geometry, type, color and
material through shared generative variables. Learn a small, interpretable combination of
these functions from actual preferences and original-design trials. Generate on the resulting
constrained surface, optimize the joint beauty prediction, and inspect the actual render.
The core chooses using its saved mathematical model; the host supplies content interpretation,
candidate proposals and implementation assistance.

## The ambition and its proof obligation

“Cannot build anything but beauty” becomes a construction contract: ordinary controls move
through coordinated variations; they cannot independently break the relationships that make
the selected composition work. Export verifies the beauty model on the rendered result.

We can prove that a generator stays inside its mathematical family. Whether that family
deserves the name beauty must be demonstrated with human appreciation of new outputs. The
plan takes responsibility for building and improving that bridge. It does not replace the
bridge with a permanent instruction to ask an AI critic whether the page looks good.

This is the corrected implementation design. No engine, website generator, public command or
dependency is installed by this planning revision. The first build is specified in chapter 4.

Planning completion and verification are recorded in [COMPLETION.md](COMPLETION.md).

**TL;DR (12th grade):** Build an instrument whose controls change many related choices together,
and whose mathematics predicts why those choices belong together. Test whether its new
compositions are beautiful, improve that mathematics, and make it the engine of generation.
