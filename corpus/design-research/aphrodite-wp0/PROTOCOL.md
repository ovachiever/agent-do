# Aphrodite WP-0, one candidate

Commission: mn-75f010. This is the bounded experiment authorized after mn-eafd32's
review and debate. It implements narrow instances of APH-01, 02, 03 and 08.
It does not implement the complete law families or commission the engine.

## Question and budget

Can an explicit, typed relationship model discriminate the existing controls,
and does a measured spacing/type relation make a falsifiable conditional prediction?
One model version, one historical scoring pass and one controlled two-by-two
comparison are authorized here. No optimizer, target search, fitted coefficients,
automatic successor, or extra law families. Implementation defects may be fixed
with a recorded amendment and complete rerun; failures of the hypothesis may not.

## Freeze and provenance

Before scoring, `bench.py freeze` copies the 64 existing observations, writes all
inferred graphs, and hashes source inputs, executable model, binding rules, this
protocol, and comparison specification. `bench.py evaluate` refuses changed inputs.
This freeze proves sequence and reproducibility within the experiment. It does
not make familiar history unseen, nor erase prior inspection of page labels.

The semantic approximation is explicit: the agent authored a finite tag/class/text
rule set. It binds DOM leaves to display, section, value, label, prose, action,
brand and detail roles, then binds typed edges by recorded containment and order.
These are NOT hand-authored human semantic graphs. Full graph exports expose every
binding, exclusion, fallback and three-node constellation. The alternative
`local_only` graph removes cross-container fallback without changing kernel targets.
Report its coverage and ranking changes, including when it becomes inapplicable.
No page ID, verdict, site name, screenshot rating, or model-call output enters the
feature computation. IDs and labels are joined only by the diagnostic reporter.

Known measurement limitation: the old probe records visual elements and text-only
leaves. A paragraph with an inline citation may be absent while its superscript
is present. It also admits some visually clipped content. Hidden-class exclusions
are declared, but this does not repair the historical observations. Report node,
edge and inapplicability counts. A successful scalar result would still not validate
these semantic assumptions.

## Complete numerical model

`model.json` is executable specification. Its eleven named kernel constants are
agent proposals, zero are fitted. The four fixed 0.25 weights add four numerical
settings (three free weights under their sum constraint). This is fifteen explicit
numerical settings, not a claim of fifteen effective statistical degrees of freedom.
The discrete ontology, class lists, regex, edge selection, averaging, channel choices,
feature forms and applicability rules are additional modeling freedom. All are
published and frozen. There are no context-specific, per-site or per-specimen targets.
The graph binder also declares the numeric HTML level identifying a display heading;
it is an extraction convention, not an extra learned beauty coefficient.

For scalar residual r and scale s, `K(r,s) = exp(-0.5*(r/s)^2)`.
For a dominant/support pair, h and b are measured CSS font sizes, and d is minimum
rectangle separation in CSS pixels. The proposed cross-channel relation is
`d = 0.5*h`. Its affinity is `K((d-0.5*h)/b, 1)`.
It is scale-invariant under a common zoom and changes when geometry and typography
stop agreeing. Whether people prefer that agreement is an empirical question.
The other targets and equations are exhaustively listed in `model.json`.

F01 averages same-role and local grouping cues; F02 couples identity with role
differentiation; F03 measures a fixed spacing/type chord; F08 couples a chord,
a distinction and a repeated support treatment across three nodes. F08's factors
are calculated from observations. None is a hand-set 0.2/0.9 fixture value.

## Prospective conditional-compatibility comparison

`comparison.json` declares a single original fictitious workshop page, constant
copy, family, palette, width and other components. Only the heading size (48 or
96px) and heading-to-description gap (24 or 48px) vary. All four combinations are
rendered at 1440x1000, with required text present and without horizontal overflow.
Human-visible pages use neutral variant labels and reveal no scores or predictions.
This is one brief, not four independent observations. Browser-derived h, b and d
are scored with the SAME F03 equation as the historical pages, using explicitly
named content roles. The primary prediction is F03, not the combined B scorer.
Both predictions are saved before any human response; report any disagreement.

Predeclared hypothesis: 48px wins at 24px spacing; 96px wins at 48px spacing.
If Erik chooses otherwise or ties either comparison, the strict human crossover
prediction fails for this brief. Unanswered choices remain pending, never failures
or implicit approval. Keeping copy fixed means it is a test of space/type fit,
not a claim about which font family is universally better.

Why a crossover matters: `a(size)+b(space)` cannot reverse the size ordering when
space changes. Its size difference is constant. Any strictly increasing transform
preserves that ordering. A product of separate positive ingredient values also
cannot do it, since its log is additive. The joint residual can reverse order.
Showing that a program reverses order proves a property of the program; a human
crossover would support this one relationship in this one brief only. It would
not prove universal beauty, taste transfer or an adequately powered study.

No human rating is requested until the complete frozen comparison is reviewable.
Human acceptance, if needed, is a separate field check with exact provenance.
