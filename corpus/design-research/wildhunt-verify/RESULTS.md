## What changes the decision

1. **Do not put any survivor into DPT: there are none.** Three Trees has the strongest registered preference result, 8/10, but p=0.0546875 already misses alpha=0.05 before multiplicity correction (Holm p=0.6015625). Its worst canon-to-negative gap is only 0.001797 against the registered 0.05 margin. Its pin gate is incomplete because historic pin DOMs are absent.
2. **Compression reuse is not enough.** Raster Self-Teaching gives broken 0.105452, above canon-today 0.027355 and canon-dossier 0.045960. The HTML channel gives barf 0.508021, above both canon fixtures. Its targeted raster ordering also fails: D1 0.028013 < B1 0.060646. The HTML channel orders that pair correctly, but fails both Null traps. The cross-round D1/B1 comparison has different capture lengths and is a supplemental falsifier only.
3. **The TSE mapping needs conceptual repair before any new taste claim.** The tested feature-covariance proxy gives broken 3.137976, above both canons (1.907127 and 1.579979), and Pete's Pasta 1.740048. A plain white image scores 2.406769, also above both canons. Shared color/gradient descriptor structure creates apparent integration even in that blank image. A static descriptor covariance is not a neural dynamical ensemble. This result kills the implemented mapping, not the neuroscience formalism.
4. **Bands can pass controls while failing taste.** The pins-only squint interval passes fixtures, both Null traps and all four available pins, but records only 1/10 strict preference wins. Most within-band specimens tie. This is why the human-feedback gate cannot be replaced by attractive fixture results.

## Provenance and limitations

- Recovered 261 source/asset versions from 141 transcript files, plus seven complete saved artifact galleries. Every source version has a hash and transcript/tool-result receipt in recovery.json. No recorded shell command was executed. Successful Write/Edit replay was exact; zero edits required guessing.
- Gallery image bytes supersede transcript thumbnails. M3's gallery image differs from its Read image; the gallery version was selected before scoring. One nominal .png Read carried JPEG bytes, which remain correctly typed and attributed.
- Auction Lot is dossier-D, verified from the original gallery title/IMG placeholder and blind key, not from a recap. Library Round 3's paneldock preference and Library Round 4's askhero win remain distinct. Round 7's conflict remains unresolved.
- Early round captures are 1440x900 viewports. Round 6 consists of full-page 1920-wide captures of different lengths. Pins are still longer full pages. The 360-pixel analysis width preserves aspect ratio but cannot remove content-length or capture-policy confounds. The 24-region TSE partition stretches across each whole capture.
- DOM measurements are fresh probes of recovered HTML, clipped to historical image extent. They are not verified pixel-identical historical browser states. Unused font subsets can remain unloaded; no font load errors or broken image loads were observed in the 64 probes. No historical DOM exists for the pin captures. No current live page was quietly substituted.
- DOM containment is not semantic truth. Minimum partition agreement does not know whether MC1's connectors represent real flow; area/heading weights do not know business importance. The implementations test declared proxies only.
- The corpus is one person's selected examples, not an independent aesthetic population. There is no learned aesthetic judge, no holdout claim for the pin-fitted bands, no manufactured uncertainty band, and no adjustment after scores.
