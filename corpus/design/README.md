# Very Good Designs — Erik-ratified exemplar corpus

Pins of designs Erik has judged very good, kept as ground truth for the taste instrument
DPT deliberately is not ("not-DPT-DPT", the moon program's taste side). DPT stays a floor
and regression instrument; this corpus is where taste labels live.

Rules:
- Only Erik pins. A pin records his words and the date. Unpinning is recorded, never deleted.
- Every pin carries captured evidence (full-page screenshot in shots/, dated — sites change),
  a computed-style probe, a dpt reading as annotation only, and a short observed-grammar note
  labeled as the agent's reading, never as Erik's.
- Contrastive value: pins sit against the killed candidates in the design-round records.
  What Erik advanced and what he killed, together, are the training signal.

Pins: pins/NNN-<slug>.md
History note: first written 2026-08-31; that uncommitted copy was cleaned from the working
tree and rebuilt 2026-09-06 (pins verbatim; shots 001–003 recaptured that day).
