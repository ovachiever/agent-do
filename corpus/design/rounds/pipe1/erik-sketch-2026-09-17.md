## Item language

An item is always presented in this order:

```text
ITEM-0417
simulating · GREENLIGHT

Fungal-network-inspired bedding amendment cuts house ammonia
24% (18–30%) at no change in litter moisture, paying back in one flock.

G12 · fitness 0.91 · biomimicry · research_backed
2 parents · seed papers · run-1842
```

The compact river row contains only what is needed to distinguish and judge it:

- Item identifier
- Current state
- Council verdict, where one exists
- Generation
- Fitness
- One-sentence thesis when the row is expanded
- Pending owner act when one is required

The state vocabulary remains exact and ordered:

```text
embryo
candidate
in_evaluation
greenlight
liminal
vetoed
simulating
pilot_ready
in_pilot
commercializing
launched
archived
failed
```

State and verdict are separate. `greenlight`, `liminal`, and `vetoed` are lifecycle states; `GREENLIGHT`, `LIMINAL`, and `VETO` are council verdicts preserved as dated events.

Generation strategy uses the supplied machine vocabulary without friendlier aliases:

```text
biomimicry
first_principles
inversion
cross_domain
constraint_relaxation
edge_case
evolutionary_recombination
speciation_exploration
mutation_amplification
fitness_landscape_climbing
diversity_maintenance
real_world_feedback
```

Generation mode is always shown as `research_backed` or `one_shot`. It is never inferred from whether papers happen to be attached.

Every value opens its receipt:

| Instrument | Opens |
|---|---|
| Fitness | Components and component history |
| Verdict | Council session and each judge's reason |
| Thesis | Claimed effect, interval and seed papers |
| Generation | Generation population, survival and best fitness |
| Strategy | Creation event, parents and mutations |
| Lineage | Parents, siblings, descendants and species |
| Simulation | Run in the Simulation room |
| Sites | Pilot deployments |
| Readings | Field observations |
| State | Dated transition event |

## Item surface

The selected item is the primary instrument. It occupies the widest pane and has five vertically ordered regions.

### Identity

The identifier, state and council verdict sit above the item's thesis. The thesis is reproduced exactly as evaluated; later wording does not silently replace it.

If the thesis changes materially, the new form is another dated version. The council event continues pointing to the text it actually judged.

### Fitness

Fitness receives the largest graphical treatment after the thesis:

```text
FITNESS

0.0                         0.5                         1.0
 ├───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┤
                                                       ● 0.91
```

The summary score opens its components. Each component opens the evidence and calculation that produced it. Changes across generations remain visible rather than replacing earlier scores.

The fitness floor sits beside the river because it acts on the displayed population. Moving it immediately changes the visible population but does not remove filtered items from the underlying record.

### Council

Council activity belongs to the item:

```text
Council       GREENLIGHT
Tue 15 Sep · 14:00

Judge 01      GREENLIGHT
"The claimed effect is bounded and testable."

Judge 02      GREENLIGHT
"The litter-moisture constraint is explicit."

Judge 03      LIMINAL
"The one-flock payback needs simulation."
```

The council result is prominent, but disagreement remains visible. Opening the verdict exposes all judges, reasons, evidence citations and the exact item version they considered.

A later council session creates another event. It does not edit or replace the first one.

### Receipts

The approved claim is decomposed without rewriting its meaning:

```text
Thesis        24%
Interval      18–30%
Constraint    no litter-moisture change
Payback       one flock
```

Papers sit directly below because they seed the thesis. Parents and siblings sit in the inspector because they explain how the machine produced the item rather than whether the claim is supported.

Once simulation begins, the surface adds:

```text
Run           run-1842
Result        pending
Interval      —
In domain     —
Hindcast      —
```

A completed run replaces the pending fields with its result, interval, domain flag and hindcast skill. The run identifier opens the full Simulation workstation.

Once a pilot begins, the same region adds sites and readings. It does not replace the simulation receipt.

### River

The selected item's location is shown on one quiet lifecycle axis:

```text
embryo  candidate  evaluation  council  simulation  pilot  commerce
  ○────────○────────○──────────●──────────◉────────○────────○
                                          item-0417
```

This axis shows position, not progress percentage. `Vetoed`, `archived` and `failed` are terminal or resting states in the record, not missing portions of an allegedly incomplete journey.

## Operator controls

Controls sit beside the instrument they affect.

### Population controls

The left pane contains:

- Fitness floor
- Stage filter
- Verdict filter
- Strategy filter
- Text search
- Newest sort
- Fitness sort

Filters change the visible river only. They do not change generation statistics or record counts.

### Item controls

The inspector contains the actions that ask the machine for more:

```text
[Breed variants]
[Commission research]
[Re-evaluate]
[Send simulation]
```

`Breed variants` creates descendants linked to the selected parent.

`Commission research` records the identified gap and creates a research request without changing the current council verdict.

`Re-evaluate` creates a new council event against a dated item version.

`Send simulation` creates or links a run and transitions the item through the applicable lifecycle state. It does not create a synthetic result before the simulator returns one.

`Open Dossier` leaves Pipeline for the one-innovation decision surface.

### Disposition controls

Parking and killing remain distinct:

```text
DISPOSITION

[Park]    [Kill]

Reason   ______________________________
Date     15 Sep 2026
Actor    Owner

[Record act]
```

Parking records a dated act and places the item in `liminal`. It preserves the gap, reason and conditions for reconsideration.

Killing records a dated act and moves the item to `vetoed`, `archived` or `failed` according to its existing lifecycle and evidence. It never erases the item.

A recorded act contains:

- Action
- Effective date
- Actor
- Reason
- Item version
- Evidence available
- Prior state
- Resulting state

Acts are append-only. Reversal creates a later act.

## Dead and parked

`Liminal` remains in the active river, visually quiet but not greyed into irrelevance:

```text
LIMINAL                                                   3
item-0418 · G12 · 0.79
Parked 15 Sep · evidence gap                            ▸
+2
```

Opening it shows the council disagreement, owner reason, missing evidence and available next acts:

```text
[Commission research]    [Re-evaluate]    [Breed variants]
```

`Vetoed` remains beside the council portion of the lifecycle. It shows the VETO event and judge reasons, and it continues contributing to generation survival and council records.

`Archived` is a preserved item no longer being advanced. Its thesis, lineage, papers, verdicts and acts remain drillable. Archiving never implies that the thesis failed.

`Failed` is reserved for an item that reached simulation, pilot or reality and did not hold. It keeps the failed result, interval, sites, readings and earlier approving decisions together.

The three therefore remain semantically different:

| State | Meaning | Record contribution |
|---|---|---|
| `liminal` | Parked pending evidence or judgment | Active population and council history |
| `vetoed` | Council or owner rejected advancement | Survival, verdict and judge records |
| `archived` | Preserved without current advancement | Population history and lineage |
| `failed` | Tested claim did not hold | Simulation, pilot and machine calibration |

Closed groups remain in the lifecycle pane with counts and one representative row. Empty stages collapse to a single quiet line:

```text
PILOT_READY                                              0
```

Nothing receives a delete action. Descendants continue pointing to vetoed, archived or failed parents, and lineage view shows them without repairing the family tree after the fact.
