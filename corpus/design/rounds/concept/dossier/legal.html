The argument

I.The mechanism is settled biology, applied at the hour the industry skips.

A hen builds her shell in the dark. Mineralization begins about five hours after the egg reaches the shell gland and runs through the night — twelve to eighteen hours in which calcium demand peaks exactly when no feed is being eaten [S2][S7]. A ration that spreads fine limestone evenly across the day delivers calcium when the hen least needs it, and leaves her to mine her own medullary bone when she needs it most.

Candidate C12-0847 moves the calcium rather than adding more. The final four feeding hours carry the day’s coarse fraction: particles sized to persist in the gizzard past midnight and dissolve across the calcification window. Split-feeding trials report crack reductions of 1.4 to 2.6 percentage points, with measurably stronger tibiae in late lay [S2]. Across 1,024 simulated flock-cycles the sequenced ration held shell breaking strength above 38 newtons through week 78 — eleven weeks past the baseline’s decline into checks and thin-shell loss [S1].

II.The arithmetic is shown, and it survives its own floor.

Exhibit A prices the mechanism against the Meadowbrook reference complex — twelve houses of 96,000 hens — using the operator’s own 52-week telemetry [S4] and the week-33 market [S3]. Every figure names its source, and the formula is printed where it can be checked.

The floor matters more than the spot. At a grade gap of $0.42 per dozen — the five-year low, touched once in 2023 — the same arithmetic returns $10,999 per house-year and repays the retrofit in fifteen months [S3]. The candidate does not need a good market. It needs a market.

III.The bird is better off whether or not the ledger notices.

Late-lay osteoporosis is the tax a hen pays for building shells out of her own skeleton. Under the sequenced ration the keel-fracture model projects 22 percent fewer new fractures after week 60 [S6], in line with the bone-strength gains both published trials measured [S2]. Nothing in the intervention handles or doses the bird: the schedule moves, and the hen keeps her own hours.

The ecological case is modest and stated at its size — 4.1 percent less limestone drawn per egg [S9], no land-use consequence either way. The ethics review found no interest of the bird’s traded against the margin: the rare candidate whose welfare term and profit term point the same direction [S8].

IV.What argues against advancement, on the record.

  1. IV.1 · Heat. Above a sustained 32 °C, afternoon intake sags and the calcium window narrows; simulated gains fall to +0.6 points [S1]. The Adversary’s condition follows: install at fall placement, never midsummer.
  2. IV.2 · The floor. At the 2023 spread, payback stretches to fifteen months. Positive — and printed rather than rounded away [S3].
  3. IV.3 · Baseline grind. One trial found no bone benefit where the ration was already coarse [S2]. Meadowbrook feeds fine grind, so the effect applies; an intake audit is written into the trial protocol regardless.
  4. IV.4 · The social mark. 58 is the lowest score on this record: one crew-day of retrofit disruption per house, and a second hopper line to keep clean [S5]. The council weighed it and left it visible.
