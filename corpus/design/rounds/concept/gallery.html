What the round discovered beyond the rankings

Emergent conventions worth keeping regardless of winner

Innovations carry names ("The Overnight Shell," "The Descent Lattice," "The Windfall Loop," "Calcium on the Night Shift") with thesis subtitles — no prompt asked for this; several builders converged on it independently. The dossier's spine as Question Presented → Holding. Per-figure provenance printed under every fitness number. Machine-log entries carrying role labels and per-entry cost. And ruling language with actual authority: "Ratified here is executed — no further confirmation." "Your greenlight commissions the dossier's 40-acre field protocol; nothing is planted until you rule."
