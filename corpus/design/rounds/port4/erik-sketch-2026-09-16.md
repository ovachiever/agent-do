## Position language

The left rail is the producer's active book, not an innovation funnel.

- `Piloting` means the intervention is operating in a limited number of houses or flocks.
- `Launched` means it is available for broader farm adoption.
- `Scaling` means deployment is expanding across houses, farms or production systems.
- `Exited` means commercial or operating involvement ended, but value may have been returned through a sale or licence.
- `Retired` means the approach was valid but superseded, consolidated or no longer useful.
- `Failed` means the approved field claim did not hold.
- `Unproven` means there is not yet enough flock, site or cycle evidence to score the claim.

Standing and performance remain separate. For example:

| Position | Production state | Owner standing | Field verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fungal ammonia reduction | Scaling | Scale | Beating thesis |
| Litter moisture control | Scaling | Hold | On thesis |
| Feed-cost proxy blending | Launched | Pause | Below thesis |
| Shell thickness via calcium timing | Piloting | Hold | Unproven |
| Manure valorization pellets | Exited | Exit | Beating thesis |
| Static ventilation schedule | Retired | Retire | On thesis |
| Probiotic water dosing | Failed | Exit | Below thesis |

That distinction prevents "failed" from becoming a vague catch-all. An intervention can work technically and still be retired, or beat its operating claim and still be exited through licensing.

## Thesis surface

Each position's approved production thesis remains frozen:

> Fungal-network-inspired bedding amendment cuts house ammonia 24% (18–30%) at no change in litter moisture, paying back in one flock.

The room then scores every material part of that statement:

- House-ammonia reduction
- Litter-moisture neutrality
- Payback period
- Mortality effect
- Number and variety of sites
- Number of valid readings
- Evidence cutoff
- Conditions under which the claim was approved

"Beating thesis" should not mean every number is favorable. Here, ammonia reduction beat the approved interval while litter moisture, payback and mortality remained inside their intervals.

Operational controls use similarly concrete language:

- Start pilot
- Add flock
- Add house
- Advance to commercial deployment
- Widen across barn types
- Pause one site
- Stop treatment
- End deployment

Every action records the actor, reason, effective date, affected sites and evidence available when the decision was made. A later decision supersedes the prior act without rewriting it.
