The owner

The owner does not operate the machine. The owner reviews winners, believes or challenges the receipts, and decides.

Every claim carries its source. Every verdict is recorded with its reason. Nothing is deleted.

Approved claims stay frozen beside what the field measured, for the rest of the bet's life.

Where the machine cannot answer, it says so: cannot simulate is a verdict, not an error.
